The short version

Anchor is a daily-structure app for adults. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Your routines, your history and your settings are stored on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

There is exactly one feature that sends anything off your device, it is off until you switch it on, and it sends one line of text and nothing else. It is described in full below.

What is stored on your device

All of it stays in the app's own storage on your device (IndexedDB, with a synchronous localStorage mirror):

  • routines, their steps and their schedules;
  • your run history — what you started, finished, skipped or put down, and how long steps took;
  • medication steps you created, and a refill unit count if you chose to keep one. Anchor counts units. It never records or suggests a dose;
  • thoughts you type into the inbox, one-off timer history, your reward list;
  • your settings, including language, theme, text size and region;
  • your plan status.

We cannot see any of it. There is no account to attach it to and no server to send it to. If you uninstall the app, it is gone; the optional backup file is the only copy that outlives the install, and you create and hold that file yourself.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics of any kind.
  • We never ask whether you have a diagnosis, and we store no such information.
  • No contacts, photos, location or microphone access.

The one thing that leaves your device: AI help

Anchor Pro includes an optional feature that asks an AI model to suggest steps for a task you are stuck on. It is switched off on every install. You turn it on yourself, under You → Your data → AI help.

While it is switched on, and only when you tap the AI button:

  • What is sent: the single line of text you typed into the Break it down box, plus the name of your chosen language.
  • Where it is sent: NVIDIA's inference API (integrate.api.nvidia.com). NVIDIA processes it under their own terms and privacy policy: nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/privacy-policy.
  • What is never sent: your routines, your step titles, your history, your run times, your inbox, your settings, your medication information, or any identifier. None of it is included in the request, and the app is built so that it cannot be.
  • A safety refusal: if the text you typed appears to contain a medicine dose (for example a number followed by mg or ml), the request is refused before it is made and the app tells you so. Nothing is sent.
  • What comes back: a suggested list of steps. It is shown to you as a suggestion which you accept or discard. It is not saved anywhere until you accept it, and it never replaces your own steps on its own.

AI-generated suggestions can be wrong while sounding confident. The app says so on screen, next to the suggestion. Nothing it suggests is medical, legal or financial advice.

If you never switch this on, Anchor makes no network request at all.

About the API key

By default the app uses a key belonging to the publisher, so the feature works without you signing up for anything. You may instead enter your own NVIDIA API key under You → Your data; if you do, yours is used instead. A key you enter is stored only on your device, is deliberately excluded from backup files, and is deleted when you remove it or erase the app's data.

Purchases

Anchor Pro is sold through Google Play. Google Play takes the payment, holds the subscription, and is where a subscription is cancelled. Anchor never asks for, sees, or stores card or payment details — there is no payment field anywhere in the app.

Google processes purchases under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Anchor stores only which plan you hold and its renewal date, on your device.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device's own alarm and notification system. Their content never leaves the device and is not sent through any push service.

Backups and export

If you export a backup, the file is written wherever you choose to save it. You can encrypt it with a passphrase, which we cannot recover. We never receive the file. Exported calendar (.ics) files work the same way.

Children

Anchor is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Your control

  • Everything the app holds can be exported from You → Your data → Export a backup.
  • Everything the app holds can be deleted from You → Your data → Erase everything here, which also deletes any API key you entered.
  • Uninstalling removes all of it.

Because there is no account and no server, there is nothing held about you for us to look up, correct or delete on request — the delete control is in your hands, in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the change will be described in the app's release notes as well as here.
